How it works

Design artwork for your Steam profile on top of your own background, in four steps.

  1. 1

    Get your files

    Download your background from steam.design as a ZIP and unzip it.

  2. 2

    Choose a showcase

    In Layout, pick one and upload its files.

    Featured Showcase

    Artwork Showcase

    Profile Picture

  3. 3

    Design

    Style sets theme and colours, Widgets adds things, Edit changes the selected one.

  4. 4

    Export

    Pick PNG, JPG or GIF (at most 5 MB per file) and save. The export window walks you through the upload to Steam.
